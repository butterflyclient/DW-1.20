REPLAY DW AUTOMATIC TOURNAMENT FORMATION
Date: 2026-10-06

Issue
- Formation searches previously required a publication listing the actual players.
- The server could send joins but could not allocate teams or complete formation.
- A party withdrawal could not release its team slot for another real party.

Target-build research
- Original Replay StartMatchmaking 0x14104A450 writes tournament.team_size.
- Original decoder 0x14213F1C0 reads event 2009 as typed UInt64 search ID and
  bracket string; event 2010 is one bracket string; event 2011 is typed UInt64
  tournament ID. No extra synthetic fields or lengths are used.
- Native bracket parser 0x141074530 consumes bounded indexed team/round arrays.
  FORMING leaves native counts unset; PLAYING processes the complete bracket.
- The IW8 source behavior oracle treats event 2011 as a formation disband. Its
  callback is not claimed as a newly verified native positive completion here.
- Wire IDs/layouts come from original Replay evidence, not another build's RVAs.

Changes
- Capacity publications specify team size and team count, without player IDs,
  team arrays, rounds or acceptance. Normal signed machine registration supplies
  the player identity; no development provisioning or seed friendship is needed.
- Authenticated party searches reserve the first team with capacity for the
  entire party. Parties are never split, and assigned slots cannot be taken over.
- The backend generates native teams/rounds from those reservations and remains
  FORMING until every team is full and every user has acknowledged through a
  still-live authenticated session. The final acknowledgement commits PLAYING
  and sends exact event-2010 documents to accepted participants.
- Readiness requires that player's accepted session and the completed bracket.
- A forming leave/decline releases the whole party allocation, removes only
  the caller's enrollment and resets peer acceptance/readiness. Event 2011 and
  a durable catch-up marker notify the peers. A fresh search clears the marker.
- Immutable competition identity/capacity is checked against the durable record.
  Publish a new tournament ID to change it. Publisher files are not rewritten.
- Enrollment, formation and search/index changes use the existing flushed
  matchmaking transaction. One fixed cross-domain enrollment filename maps to
  its known path. Whole snapshots are validated before recovery writes.
- An interrupted acknowledgement rolls forward enrollment and formation together.
  Notification source failure cannot uncouple a valid forming-party release.
- No game recompile, proxy, additional thread, listener or external port added.
