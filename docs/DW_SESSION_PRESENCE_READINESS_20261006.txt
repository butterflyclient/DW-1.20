REPLAY DW LOBBY SESSION/PRESENCE READINESS
2026-10-06

Issue
Revoked idle lobby sockets could retain online status for up to ten minutes.
The native task loop checked credentials only after frame() returned, while
the framing loop handled keepalives internally. Incomplete reads retained
the same endpoint until the receive deadline.

Research
General online lookup counted the connection registry without validating its
issuer leases. Host-specific sessionOnline already validated the issuer outside
the registry lock. The transport polls recv every second, so existing polling
can retire revoked sessions without a new background manager or wire protocol.
Normal endpoint teardown already owns channel cleanup and revision-protected,
scoped native presence notifications.

Fix
Online queries require at least one registered, valid issuer identity.
After the original handshake verifies its challenge, transport read waits check
that identity's lease before recv, on polling and after completing bytes.
Revoked idle, keepalive and partial reads return through normal endpoint teardown.
Disconnect handling counts only other valid endpoints for that title/account.
Duplicate sockets and independently signed sessions remain online until their
last valid connection disconnects. Windows FIN and unread-data connection-reset
responses both count as disconnection in the fixture; neither permits an echo
or successful native task result.
