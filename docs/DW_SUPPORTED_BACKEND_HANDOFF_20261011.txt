REPLAY DW SUPPORTED EMULATION HANDOFF
2026-10-11 UTC

Scope
The owner permits exclusions where the original data or target contract does
not support faithful emulation. This release implements the verified contracts
and preserves explicit failures for unsupported operations. It does not claim
a complete audit of every indirect SDK call or a new live-game callback test.

Changes
71 mission stages were added in the two integration batches: original kill
modifiers/rarity/circle context, 29 MP attachment/streak/field stages, 12 BR
placement/downed stages, nine qualified match completion/win stages, and four
Ground War stages. There are now 181 earning stages among the 232 canonical
definitions. All definitions remain available; 51 earning predicates are
excluded with individual IDs, descriptions, missing contracts, and evidence.

MP terminal streak reports contain kills in Param1, rather than duration.
Field-upgrade results retain their original meanings: resupplies, marks, hits,
projectiles, weapons given, successful insertion, or damage. Cover damage is
not a use count. Attachment matching follows five native 12-bit slots and the
recognized count, preserving no-attachment and unknown values.

The original qualified outcomes are 1=win, 2=loss, 3=tie. Undefined/unclassified
0 does not complete a mission. The native FFA win classification is qualified
top three. Specific-mode wins use the exact original game-type identity.

Stock start_match reports bind to their authenticated current public allocation
without requiring custom dw_match_id fields. Tagged starts still require the
explicit matching lobby. Stock terminal reports resolve only one eligible
captured native start in that same allocation/session. Ambiguity earns nothing.
The first qualified end and first placement are durable and cannot be replayed
under a new request ID. A late placement bounded by the recorded end can arrive
after the end report. Stage activation and closed-match cutoffs remain enforced.
Legacy nine/ten-field match records remain valid; the optional placement record
adds rank and event timestamp to the full validated layout.

Ground War is bound by the original game-type table and independent Localize
and playlist evidence to arm, mode 32. The .50 GS is original weapon ID 3.
Ordinary Domination/TDM packets and another pistol do not substitute for it.

Exclusions
Six native routes remain explicitly unavailable: 50/22, 58/22, 193/9, 193/10,
193/12, and 193/24. Their original response reader/ownership contracts remain
unbound. They return error 108 with no results. Unknown valid REST operations
return status 503 and LocalServiceUnavailable; the connection remains usable.
No ordinary DTO is substituted for an unverified extended result layout.

The 51 excluded mission predicates need original CP operation ownership,
specific vehicle/destruction identity, occurrence/life/window consumption,
Scavenger or successful hack semantics, Kill Chain or faction selection, or
exact named attachment data. Missing native callback testing is a separate
gate and is not used as an impossibility claim. The inventory also documents
the partial native alternatives for generic field-upgrade and suppressor rules.
