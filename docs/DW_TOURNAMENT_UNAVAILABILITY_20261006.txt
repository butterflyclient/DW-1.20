REPLAY DW BACKEND: MANAGED PUBLICATION WITHDRAWAL AND EXPIRY
2026-10-06

ISSUE AND RESEARCH
The idle publisher notified Replay when a tournament disappeared or expired,
but that notification did not retire managed searches, round lobbies or account
assignments. Pending registrations could also miss the unavailable notification.
These stale assignments could survive the competition and interfere with later
matchmaking. A missing slot in one player's bracket is different from global
publication withdrawal and must not cancel a live competition.

The existing managed formation, result journal, retirement planner and recoverable
matchmaking/enrollment transaction already provided the required complete layouts.
The fix extends those mechanisms. It does not add generic success replies or alter
native game fences, renderer logic, task state, scores or winner selection.

IMPLEMENTATION
Globally withdrawn/expired managed publications get an exact validated terminal
record: schema, title, id, expires, actor, action, recorded and full native state.
The system action is publication-unavailable with actor zero. Ordinary participant
leave/decline still requires a real competing member and the original time bounds.
Expiry may be recorded after the pinned deadline. Malformed terminals fail closed.

The terminal, owned search/lobby retirement, search/account indexes and cleared
readiness commit through the existing flushed transaction. Forming searches not
yet assigned to a bracket are included. Exact IDs preserve unrelated competitions
and newer assignments. Real result journals are retained unchanged. A republished
terminal ID cannot resurrect the old competition; a new competition needs a new ID.

Native event 2012 is available to accepted and pending registrations after the
durable transition. Queued stale joins are revalidated. Cleanup runs on an enrolled
client's idle publisher or authenticated tournament request. All-offline background
sweeping, retail forfeits and disconnect timeout policy remain separate work.
