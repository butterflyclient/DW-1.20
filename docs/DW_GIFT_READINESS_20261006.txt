local signed realm. Mixed self/gift orders debit only the buyer and grant each
order's exact published items/currencies to its recipient. Native coupon owner
is the recipient and buyer is the authenticated purchaser. The buyer owns the

checksums. Full preflight validates title, buyer, unique numeric owners, inventory
layouts, receipts and conflicting disk edits before any checkpoint is replaced.

Failed checkpoints block inventory/wallet access until roll-forward can finish.
Recovery preserves metadata and the exact response; it does not debit or grant
again. An applied marker carries no snapshots and never replays.
The existing shared inventory reader/receipt validator is reused. No engine
recompile, proxy DLL, new port, account provisioning or initial friendships.
