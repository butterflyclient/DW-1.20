Confirmed audit fixes

1. Achievement completion now publishes a flushed, checksummed empty applied
   intent after all checkpoints finish. A filesystem unlink is no longer the
   sole evidence that the transaction finished. Reads recover unfinished work.
   The old-release rollback helper validates and archives the applied marker
   while the service is stopped; it refuses unsafe prepared user_state work.

2. Full progression reset includes both achievement user-state namespaces in
   the same intent as XP, states, inventory and leaderboards. This clears stale
   tutorial, boost and Battle Pass ownership keys. Account reads recover first,
   before loading user-state. Derived Battle Pass ownership is rebuilt from
   current authoritative activation states rather than stale ownership flags.

3. Public and presence joins reserve requested bot slots together with existing
   humans and guests. Capacity stays at the native playlist value. Rejected
   over-capacity joins do not change lobby documents or the requested bot count.

4. REST status and ObjectStore timestamp/extra-data fields are checked before
   narrowing conversions. Wrapped or unrepresentable values cannot appear as
   valid success codes or publication metadata. Dispatch refreshes the signed
   platform lease after obtaining the store lock, preventing a queued revoked
   session from mutating state.

5. Unchanged effective presence still saves its fresh timestamp, but it does not
   advance the notification revision or send another status push. The DW game
   setter announces a status only after a successful LUI event, retries a failed
   event, and continues native social presence scheduling on every call.

6. Original Replay buildAuthReq calls the auth-address getter before the port
   getter. Previously, local address initialization in the port override came
   too late for the first hostname choice. The rebuilt address getter now returns
   the validated INI host before that choice. The literal 15000ms auth timeout
   explains the prior first-request delay. Native-provider address/port reads
   remain native. A new game startup timing measurement has not been performed.
