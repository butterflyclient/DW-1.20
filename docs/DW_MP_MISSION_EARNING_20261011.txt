EVIDENCE AND RESULT LAYOUT

The original untouched Replay image SHA256 is
68fb1cbcb2924182724004039de55a4c50152bb6561803c4898b7930b38132f0.
The research tool executes its original ProcessParams instructions at
0x140F35980..0x140F385DC using the established CRT/asset boundaries.
It does not reproduce packing with a backend encoder.

Param3 in kill packets holds five complete 12-bit attachment ordinals plus
one. Param5 low byte holds the recognized count; 255 means no_attachments.
Tests cover changed order, the fifth slot, unknown references, the sentinel,
wrong victim types, missing Hipfire, and a wrong SMG weapon class.
The upper four bits cannot establish a complete sixth attachment ID.

Original global_mp ScriptFile 6273 recordkillstreakendstats sends
streakname,duration,endtime,kills,0,0,0. The original parser emits
streakID,kills,0,0,0,duration,endtime. Never credit duration as kills.
All 37 shipped streak table IDs were exercised; specialist perk grants
70033..70036 are excluded from called-killstreak credit.
The retained English Localize tail independently resolves Personal Radar,
Shield Turret, Cluster Strike, Wheelson, Precision Airstrike and Chopper
Gunner names, including checked prior-string aliases.

field_end emits superID,result,operators. The result has distinct semantics:
ammo box uses, recon marks, EMP hits, weapon gifts, trophy stops, Dead Silence
kills, Stopping Power kills, successful insertion 0/1, and cover damage.
Cover damage earns one deployment; it never earns that many cover uses.
A destroyed insertion with result 0 does not earn successful insertion stage
10022, but its deployment still satisfies generic field-upgrade use 1566.
The helper rejects signed-wrap and oversized counters and unknown super IDs.

Native producer payloads and their export-index hashes are retained privately
under data/remaining-mp-missions-20261011/native-producers. Public proofs are
validation/20261011-replay-mp-mission-events.json and
validation/20261011-replay-mp-mission-helper.json. The former retains the
original source-zone/export/payload/decompiled-source evidence chain.
