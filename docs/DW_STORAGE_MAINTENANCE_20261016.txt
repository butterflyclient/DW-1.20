REPLAY DW DROPLET STORAGE MAINTENANCE - 2026-10-16

What runs
replay-dw-maintenance.timer (daily 07:30 UTC, before the 08:00 backup) runs
/opt/replay-dw/maintain-storage.py --apply. Without --apply it only reports.
Installed by deploy/linux/install-service.sh with the backup units.

Retention rules (only these paths are ever touched)
  updates/map-incoming-<ns>   failed publication staging older than 24 h
  updates/images              keep the catalog's latest + two newest others
  updates/patches             keep those listed in the v1 catalog.json
  updates/assets              keep those referenced by full-image-catalog.json,
                              custom-map-catalog.json or a retained map backup
  updates/map-backups         keep the newest three
  update blobs                never removed before they are 48 h old
  /var/backups/.../before-*   ad-hoc pre-deployment backups: seven days
  /tmp/replay-dw-*            release build jobs: two days, unless in use
  /opt/replay-dw/releases     the active release + five newest others
Daily state-*.tar.gz and updates-* snapshots: seven days (backup-state.sh).

Why this is safe
Clients are offered only the full-image catalog's latest image; the updater
derives its asset and map lists from that target image, and v1 clients only
the patches listed in catalog.json. Update decisions are made under
updates/publication.lock, which the publishers hold, and a missing or
malformed catalog skips update pruning entirely. A pruned update blob remains
in the daily hard-link snapshot for seven days; its space is freed when that
snapshot expires. Republishing an older EXE or map uploads its files again.

Checks
  systemctl list-timers 'replay-dw-*'
  journalctl -u replay-dw-maintenance   (JSON summary per run)
  python3 /opt/replay-dw/maintain-storage.py   (dry run, any time)
The unit exits 2 (visible in systemctl --failed) when free space is under 15%.
