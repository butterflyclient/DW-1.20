REPLAY DW BACKEND - MANAGED TOURNAMENT WITHDRAWAL

Backend-only implementation. Replay game executable, INI, private identity,
client package, unrelated compiler changes and external port mappings are unchanged.

ISSUES AND FIX
An active participant could leave a PLAYING competition without retiring its
round lobbies or party searches. Completed competitions also retained old lobby
assignments, which blocked later competitions from allocating their first round.

The local realm now cancels an unfinished backend-managed tournament when a
still-competing participant explicitly leaves (145/22) or declines (145/23 false).
Cancellation retains a complete validated terminal record and commits enrollment,
lobby/search retirement and account indexes through one recoverable transaction.
Remaining accepted participants receive Replay's native event 2012, including
when live_updates is disabled or no round has been allocated.

Eliminated participants can leave without cancelling other teams. Their completed
transport is retired while the winner's party search remains usable. Departure
after a finished final releases stale assignments without discarding actual results.
Retirement validates every lobby-to-player/search binding before changing state.
Malformed sources do not silently remove enrollment during a failed cancellation.
An interrupted publication rolls forward the same cancellation on owned retry.
Newer unrelated assignments are preserved by matching exact lobby/search IDs.

Temporary connection loss is not withdrawal. Cancellation does not synthesize a
retail forfeit winner, score or timeout rule. Legacy manually supplied brackets
retain their existing behavior. Cancelled IDs cannot be reopened; publish a new ID.
